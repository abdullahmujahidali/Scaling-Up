# How To Use — Simple Guide

Easy steps. No tech knowledge needed.

---

## 1. How to open the course

**On your Mac (works offline — no internet needed):**

1. Open **Finder** (the smiley face icon in your dock).
2. Go to this folder: `Desktop → Projects → learning`
3. Double-click the file called **`index.html`**
4. It opens in your web browser. Done.

This is the **home page**. Start here.

**On your phone or online (needs internet):**

Open this link: **https://abdullahmujahidali.github.io/Scaling-Up/**

---

## 2. How to move around (navigation)

Three easy ways:

- **Big red button** on the home page → click **"▶ Lesson 1"** to start.
- **Left sidebar** (dark panel on the left) → click any lesson name to jump there.
- **Bottom of each lesson** → **← Prev** and **Next →** buttons walk you forward one lesson at a time.

Just start at Lesson 1 and keep clicking **Next →**. That's the whole thing.

---

## 3. How to switch to Roman Urdu

- Look at the **bottom-right corner** of any page.
- There is a **red round button** that says **🌐 Roman Urdu**.
- **Click it** → the whole page changes to Roman Urdu (English written in normal letters, like texting).
- **Click it again** → back to English.
- Your choice is remembered on every page.

> Roman Urdu = Urdu in English letters. Example: "Yeh cheez logon ko phansati hai..."
> Technical words (database, cache, server) stay in English — that's normal, real devs talk like this.

---

## 4. The lessons — how they are organized

There are **27 lessons** in total, in three groups:

### Week 1 (do these first, one per day)
The basics: Framework, Consistency, Failover, Nines, Load Balancers, Caching, CAP, SQL vs NoSQL, Replication, Databases, Indexes, N+1, Queues.

### Week 2 (do these after Week 1)
DNS & CDN, Transactions, Microservices, API Design, async, and a full "design a URL shortener" practice.

### Offline Pack (extra lessons — read anytime, good for the plane)
Rate Limiting, WebSockets, Kafka, Circuit Breakers, Idempotency, Connection Pooling, Denormalization, Search.

**You don't have to remember this list** — the sidebar and the home page show everything. Just start at Lesson 1 and use **Next →**.

---

## 5. How each lesson works

Every lesson is short (~8 minutes) and has:

- **A 30-second recall** at the top — try to remember the last lesson first.
- **A simple story** (like a shop, a restaurant, a whiteboard) to explain the idea.
- **A "Django bridge"** — how it connects to your real Python/Django work.
- **Quizzes** — click the answer, get instant feedback. **Do these!** Answering from memory is what makes it stick.
- **"Your win today"** — the one thing you learned.

---

## 6. Tip for the plane ✈️

- The files on your Mac **work with NO internet.** Just open `index.html` from Finder.
- Before you fly, if you want the phone version too: run `git push origin main` and it goes live online.
- On the plane: open `index.html`, click **▶ Lesson 1**, use **Next →**, and click **🌐 Roman Urdu** whenever a sentence is hard.

---

## If something looks broken

Press **Cmd + Shift + R** (hold Command + Shift, then press R). This forces the page to reload fresh. Fixes 99% of "it's not working" problems.
