🎯 The 5 steps for EVERY question
  1. Read the last line first: MOST cost-effective? LEAST effort? FIRST? Select TWO?
  2. Find the NEED in the story ("must not miss", "rotate", "explain why").
  3. Name the trap before you look at the options ("this is overfit").
  4. Say your answer in your head, then find it.
  5. 10-second check: does my answer do the need, or does it just share a word with the story?
🧮 Token maths with no calculator
Ignore "per million" — it is the same on both sides. Just multiply tokens × price and compare.
1,500 input × $1 = 1,500 · 200 output × $5 = 1,000 → cutting input saves more.
Cost of one request? (2,000 × $2 + 500 × $10) = 9,000 → divide by 1,000,000 → $0.009.
