Lesson A4 · IAM — policy evaluation

The cheapest point on the exam. A one-line rule (explicit Deny always wins) and a question on it nearly every time. Plus: the answer is always "remove the key, use a role".

Lesson A5 · SQS vs SNS vs Kinesis

Tell the whole messaging family apart, plus the exam's favourite pattern (SNS + SQS fan-out). And the real cause of duplicate processing — the visibility timeout, which is exactly your Celery problem.

Lesson A6 · Deployment strategies

Domain 3's most-asked question. Blue/green, canary, linear, all-at-once — and the phrase that signals each. Plus the buildspec vs appspec trap.

WHAT, WHERE, WHO — three tools, three different questions. The memory-metric trap (there is no memory without the CloudWatch Agent) and the error codes: 429, 502, 504.

✅ Exam-day checklist

  • Read the last line first — find the qualifying word (MOST cost-effective? LEAST operational overhead?).
  • Cut the two clearly wrong options — your odds go from 25% to 50%.
  • No question gets more than 2 minutes — flag it, move on, come back at the end.
  • Never leave a question blank — there's no penalty for a wrong answer. Always guess.
  • "Serverless / managed" is usually right when the question says "least operational overhead".
  • Any option storing an access key is almost always wrong — an IAM role is right.
  • Read every option to the end — AWS plants 90%-correct options with one wrong word ("ECS with EC2 launch type").
  • If a question seems bizarre — it may be one of the 15 unscored ones. Guess, move on, don't let it rattle you.
