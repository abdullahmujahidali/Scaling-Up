# Mission expanded: two alternating tracks (System Design + Backend Depth)

The learner asked to bring in deeper Python/Django and database material alongside system design. We agreed to widen the mission from "system design only" to TWO tracks that alternate: **Track A — System Design** (the Primer, in order) and **Track B — Backend Depth** (Python internals, Django/ORM depth, database internals). Confirmed with the learner. See [[MISSION.md]].

**Steering decisions the learner made:**
- Tracks **alternate** — one system-design lesson, then one depth lesson. (Interleaving also aids retention — a bonus.)
- First Track B topic chosen by learner: **Python internals** (GIL, memory, generators, async).

**Why this change:** the learner's real fear is that AI writing their code is eroding their own fundamentals/recall. "Top 1% backend" needs depth (why code/DB behave as they do), not just whiteboard design. Track B directly rebuilds that. All existing lesson design rules still apply — one concept per lesson, analogy-first, 30-sec recall warm-up. See [[0004-lessons-too-dense-go-smaller]], [[0002-retention-is-the-real-goal]].
