How to book the exam — step by step

This takes 10–15 minutes. Below is the whole process, plus the decisions you'll be asked to make and what to answer. Everything here was verified against AWS's own pages (3 Aug 2026).

~15 minutes Do this first, before studying Verified against AWS docs
Understand this first — booking is not a risky move People avoid booking because they think "what if I'm not ready?". The reality: you can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment, and cancelling more than 24 hours ahead refunds your fee. So booking a date carries no risk — only the upside that a booked date is what actually gets the studying finished. One limit to remember: you can only reschedule twice, so choose thoughtfully — but do choose today.
⭐⭐ MOST IMPORTANT — DO THIS BEFORE BOOKING You can get 30 EXTRA minutes, purely because English is not your first language. It's called "ESL +30 MINUTES" (ESL = English as a Second Language).

That means 160 minutes instead of 130. Instead of 2 minutes per question, you get 2 minutes 27 seconds. For you — whose single biggest obstacle is reading long English — this is the largest advantage available anywhere in this course. And it is completely free.

⚠️ But the catch: you must request it BEFORE booking the exam. If you book first, it won't apply to that booking. That's why this is Step 0, not Step 1.

This is not cheating or a favour — it's AWS's own official accommodation, created for exactly this. People who don't request it lose out only because nobody told them.

0Request ESL +30 minutes (before booking!)

Just five steps, done once — after which it applies permanently to every AWS exam you ever take:

1. Sign in at aws.training/Certification
2. Click "Go to your Account"
3. Click "Request Exam Accommodations", then "Request Accommodation"
4. In the Accommodation Type dropdown, select "ESL +30 MINUTES"
5. Click "Create"

That's it. No proof, no test, no documentation is required. Wait for approval (usually quick), then book the exam.

What those 30 minutes actually mean for you Remember the two-pass method: pass 1 over everything, pass 2 on the marked ones. In 130 minutes that's tight. In 160 minutes it's comfortable — roughly 105 minutes for pass 1 and 45 for pass 2.

And something bigger: when time is short people read fast, and reading fast is exactly how you miss the one word that decides the answer (MOST cost-effective, LEAST operational overhead). These 30 minutes buy you the right to read carefully — and rushing is the single biggest cause of your avoidable mistakes.

The booking steps

1Go to your AWS Certification Account

Open aws.training/certification

⚠️ Important: this is a completely separate account from the AWS console you use at work (where Lambda and S3 live). Certification has its own account. If you've never made one, create it with an Amazon account or email — a 2-minute job.

2Enter your name exactly as it appears on your ID

⚠️ This is the most common reason people get turned away on exam day. The name on your account must match your government ID (passport / CNIC) exactly — spelling, order, everything. If the ID says "Abdullah Mujahid Ali", the account must say that, not "Abdullah Mujahidali".

Go and get your ID now, and type the name while looking at it — not from memory.

3Go to Schedule New Exam

Inside the account: Certification → Schedule New Exam. Then pick "AWS Certified Developer – Associate" from the list (code: DVA-C02).

Direct link: cp.certmetrics.com/amazon/en/schedule/schedule-exam

Make sure you select DVA-C02 — not SAA-C03 (Solutions Architect). The names look alike.

4Decision: online from home, or a test centre?

My recommendation — and why Pick a test centre if there's one in your city. The reason is your English constraint: your brain will already be working harder because you're reading long questions in a second language. An online exam adds load on top — watching the connection, the room, answering the proctor. At a centre you do one thing: take the exam.

If the centre is far or costly, online is genuinely fine — just run the system test (Pearson VUE provides one) the day before, and tell your household nobody enters for 2.5 hours.

5Pick the date: Friday 21 August 2026

This date sits exactly at the end of your 3-week plan, and being a Friday it leaves the next day free.

Which time? Whenever your mind is freshest — for most people, morning. Don't pick an evening slot; 130 minutes of reading English on a tired brain is punishing. A 9 or 10 am slot is ideal.

6Before paying — do this one thing

⚠️ Before you pay, ask Abdullah Ajmal whether the company reimburses the 150 USD or holds vouchers. Many companies do, and AWS partner companies get discount vouchers. That one message could save you 150 dollars.

If he doesn't reply today, don't let it block the booking — book anyway, because cancelling more than 24 hours ahead is fully refunded. If a voucher turns up, cancel and rebook with it.

7Book it, and keep the confirmation email

After payment, Pearson VUE emails a confirmation with the date, time, and (for a centre) the address. Star or save that email. It also contains the exam-day link if you chose online.

Then tell me the date you booked and I'll adjust the plan to match.

What to bring on exam day

  • ⭐ Two forms of ID. Primary: government-issued photo ID (a passport is best). Secondary: anything bearing your name (bank card, credit card). Both must show the exact name used in the booking.
  • If in doubt about local ID, take your passport — AWS states that without a qualifying local ID, an international passport is required as the primary ID.
  • Arrive 30 minutes early. Late arrivals are refused entry and the fee is forfeited.
  • Nothing else. Phone, watch, paper, water — all go in a locker. The centre provides something to write on.
Two things people forget (1) The name mismatch. If the booking name and ID name differ, you're refused entry and the fee is lost. Check today, not on exam day.

(2) The 24-hour rule. Inside 24 hours you can neither cancel nor reschedule — and not showing up forfeits the whole fee. If you need to move the date, decide at least 2 days ahead.

Verified facts about the exam

  • Cost: 150 USD.
  • 65 questions, 130 minutes — or 160 minutes with ESL +30. Only 50 are scored; 15 are experimental.
  • Pass = 720/1000, compensatory scoring (you needn't pass each domain separately).
  • Languages: English, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese, Spanish (Latin America). No Urdu — which is exactly why every practice question here is in English.
  • You may reschedule only twice. Free up to 24 hours before.
  • After a fail, you wait 14 days before retaking. (Not the end of the world — plenty of people pass on the second attempt.)
