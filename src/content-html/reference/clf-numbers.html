AWS CLF-C02 · Reference · Cheat Sheet

CLF Numbers & Trigger Words

Cloud Practitioner asks for far fewer numbers than DVA — it mostly asks "which service". So this sheet has two halves: the few numbers that genuinely appear, and the words that point straight at an answer. Given the English, the second half matters more for you.

Print this one ⭐ = most frequently tested
How to use this sheet Don't try to memorise the whole sheet at once. Do the ⭐ rows first. Look at it daily, but test yourself first, then look — reading doesn't build memory, trying to recall does.

Specifically for you: the "Trigger Words" table below will pay the most. It maps the exam's English directly onto answers. Learn only that table and you'll get many questions right even without fully parsing the sentence.

⭐ Trigger words — from word to answer

AWS questions are long, but usually one word decides the answer. The rest is just scenery. This table is those words.

If the question saysThe answer isWhy
"designated TAM"Enterprise SupportOnly Enterprise gives a personal TAM. On-Ramp gives a pool.
"24×7 support, lowest cost"Business SupportThe first plan with 24×7 phone and chat.
"fault-tolerant", "can be interrupted"Spot InstancesUp to 90% off. Only when work can stop.
"steady state", "predictable"Reserved / Savings PlansAlways-on work = commit and save.
"unpredictable", "short-term"On-DemandDon't commit to what you can't predict.
"alert when spending exceeds"AWS BudgetsBudgets looks forward; Cost Explorer looks back.
"analyse past costs"Cost ExplorerWhat's already spent. Also a 12-month forecast.
"estimate before building"Pricing CalculatorAn estimate before anything exists.
"unknown/changing access pattern"S3 Intelligent-TieringAWS moves tiers for you automatically.
"archive", "retain 7 years"S3 Glacier Deep ArchiveCheapest storage; retrieval takes hours.
"BYOL", "licensing", "compliance"Dedicated HostsOnly these two reasons justify it.
"highly available"Multiple AZsHA = multiple AZs. Multiple Regions = DR or latency.
"global users, reduce latency"CloudFrontThe CDN — content cached near users.
"who did what", "audit"CloudTrailCloudTrail = who/when (audit). CloudWatch = how it's performing (metrics).
"metrics", "alarms", "monitoring"CloudWatchHealth and performance monitoring.
"report abuse"AWS Trust & SafetyA small question, but it appears.
"is AWS itself down?"AWS Health DashboardAWS's own outages and their effect on your account.
