How to use this (the easy way)

No tech knowledge needed. Just follow these steps.

Want Roman Urdu? Press the 🌐 Roman Urdu button in the bottom-right corner. The whole page turns to Roman Urdu. Press again for English. Try it right now!

1. How to open the course

1

On your Mac (works even without internet)

Finder → go to Desktop → Projects → learning → double-click index.html. It opens in your browser. Done.

2

On your phone or online (needs internet)

Open this link:

Scaling Up — Home · abdullahmujahidali.github.io/Scaling-Up

2. How to move around

Three easy ways 1. Big red button — on the home page, click "▶ Lesson 1" to start. 2. Left sidebar — click any lesson in the dark panel on the left. 3. At the bottom of each lesson — use ← Prev and Next → to go one lesson at a time. Just start at Lesson 1 and keep clicking Next →.

3. How the lessons are organized

There are 27 lessons in three groups. You don't need to memorize this — the sidebar and home page show everything.

  • Week 1 — the basics (Framework, Consistency, CAP, Databases, Indexes, Queues...). Do these first, one a day.
  • Week 2 — DNS, Transactions, Microservices, API Design, async, and a full design practice. Do after Week 1.
  • Offline Pack — 8 extra lessons (Rate Limiting, WebSockets, Kafka, Circuit Breakers, Idempotency, Connection Pooling, Denormalization, Search). Read anytime — great for the plane.
Important I did not touch Week 1 and Week 2 — they are exactly the same. The 8 new lessons are a separate group after them — nothing was inserted in between.

4. How each lesson works

Each lesson is short (~8 min) and has:

  • A 30-second recall — try to remember the last lesson first.
  • A simple story (a shop, restaurant, whiteboard) to explain the idea.
  • A Django bridge — how it connects to your real Python/Django work.
  • Quizzes — click an answer, get instant feedback. Do these! Answering from memory is what makes it stick.
If something looks broken Press Cmd + Shift + R (hold Command + Shift, then press R). This reloads the page fresh. Fixes 99% of "it's not working" problems.
